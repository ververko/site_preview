About

Driven by an ongoing artistic practice and a deep foundation in visual culture, Vera Korman brings over fifteen years of experience in higher education, curatorial practice, and cultural programming. She translates broad technical, theoretical, and creative expertise into engaging courses, exhibitions, and public programs.

Her professional background includes lecturing and curriculum development at art academies, curating six editions of the Musraramix Festival, presenting work at venues such as the Tel Aviv Museum of Art and Museum Boijmans Van Beuningen, and collaborating with the Israel Festival.

Based in Barcelona, Vera designs independent educational programs, philosophical walking tours, and specialized workshops, collaborating with universities, cultural organizations, corporate clients, and global audiences.
