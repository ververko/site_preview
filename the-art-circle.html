What The Art Circle is

The Art Circle is an independent platform in Barcelona dedicated to close reading, critical thought, and direct encounters with contemporary visual culture.

Each Circle focuses on a central theme or pressing question shaping contemporary art today, such as post-digital realities, spatial politics, identity, or the conditions of artistic production itself. Over three interconnected sessions, we navigate the subject in three distinct ways:

  • Theoretical Frameworks & History: Mapping the key concepts, critical texts, and historical shifts that inform the topic.
  • Conversations with Practitioners: Direct exchanges with curators, artists, collectors, and thinkers working in the field.
  • Site-Responsive & Behind-the-Scenes Encounters: Going beyond gallery walls into artist studios, closed archives, museum storages, private libraries, and conservation labs, experiencing the material and institutional mechanisms of art firsthand.

Designed for an engaged audience, The Art Circle offers a space for sustained critical discourse and shared aesthetic experience.
