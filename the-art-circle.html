What The Art Circle is

Art begins with looking, deepens through experience, and grows into a conversation with the present.

The Art Circle is an independent forum in Barcelona dedicated to engaging closely with contemporary art and visual culture. Each circle explores a broad theme across three monthly sessions. Each session approaches the theme from a different angle and uses a distinct methodology, combining historical context, fieldwork, direct dialogue with local practitioners, and collective discussion over shared meals.

We explore how the art of our time actively shapes the way we think, see, and inhabit the world.
