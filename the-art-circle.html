About the Course

What The Art Circle is

Explore the worlds that art opens.

The Art Circle brings together art, ideas and the people who shape them. We look closely at artworks, move through their histories and contexts, and follow the questions they open — about taste, value, identity, power, culture and the world we live in.

Three thematic circles unfold across the year, each built around a different question and a different way into art. One month might begin with an exhibition, another with a lecture, a gallery walk, a museum, an artist or a conversation with someone from inside the art world. Always, the evening moves between looking, thinking, meeting and experiencing — and continues into the life of Barcelona, over a table, a glass of wine and a good conversation.
