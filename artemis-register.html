The Art Question #1

The Artistic Gesture

Ever wondered why a urinal ended up in a museum? Or how a banana taped to a wall sold for millions? Join us for a live morning at the Fundació Joan Brossa, home of the Barcelona poet who turned everyday objects into art.

The morning includes
  • A welcome buffet alongside two exhibitions in the Fundació's galleries
  • A private screening of video works that test the limits of art
  • The Waiting Artist, a live performance by Hannah Berestizhevsky
  • A talk by Vera Korman, from Duchamp to Cattelan's banana
  • A conversation around the table

No background needed. Just curiosity.

Date & Place

Wednesday, 4 November, 11:00 to 14:00
Fundació Joan Brossa, Barcelona
In English

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How do I register?

Write to verakorman@gmail.com or message us on WhatsApp. Space is limited, so we recommend registering early.

Do I need to attend all the sessions of a cycle?

No. Every session stands on its own and takes a different angle on the cycle's theme, so you can join any session, or as many as you like. Each one opens for registration separately.

Do I need a background in art?

No background needed. Just curiosity.

What language are the sessions in?

The Artistic Gesture is held in English.

What does the fee cover?

Everything in the morning's program, as listed above: the welcome buffet, the two exhibitions, the private screening, the live performance, the talk, and the conversation around the table.

What if I can't make it after registering?

Cancellations made at least 7 days before the session are refunded in full. After that, the fee is non-refundable, but your spot can be transferred to someone else at no charge.

When are the next sessions?

The Museum & The Market and The Curator's Eye are coming next. Dates will be announced soon.
